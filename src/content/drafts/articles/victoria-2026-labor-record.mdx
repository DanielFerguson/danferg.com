<h2 id="the-years-behind-this-election" tabindex="-1">The years behind this election</h2>

Labor’s record stretches across Daniel Andrews, Jacinta Allan and now Ben Carroll. Carroll became Premier on 28 July 2026 when Allan resigned ahead of a spill she was expected to lose, amid allegations of criminal infiltration of government construction sites and polling that pointed to a Liberal–One Nation government <Cite k={["vic-premier", "abc-carroll"]} />. Caucus elected him unanimously, after a brief second nomination was withdrawn. His first act was to call a royal commission into construction integrity; Christopher Kourakis, a former South Australian chief justice, was appointed on 20 August and reports in August 2027, well after the election <Cite k="abc-kourakis" />.

So the person asking for a fourth term has held the job for weeks and inherited nearly twelve years of decisions. That calls for care about personal credit and blame in both directions. The timeline below is banded by premier for that reason, and where a finding arrived long after the conduct it shows both dates.

<h2 id="transport" tabindex="-1">Transport people can use</h2>

The Metro Tunnel opened in November 2025 with five new stations, and the full timetable switch followed in February 2026 <Cite k="metro-tunnel" /> <Tag kind="delivered" />. The West Gate Tunnel opened in December 2025, three years late and tolled <Cite k="west-gate-tunnel" /> <Tag kind="delivered" />. By 1 September this year 94 level crossings were gone, against a target of 110 <Cite k="melton-94" /> <Tag kind="delivered" />. These are real changes to the network. Whether they were worth what they cost is a separate argument.

Outside Melbourne the fare cap matters more: since March 2023 a regional daily fare has cost no more than a metropolitan one <Cite k="pto-fare-cap" /> <Tag kind="delivered" />. Under-18s have travelled free with a Youth myki since January 2026, and free weekend travel was widened statewide to seniors, carers and Disability Support Pension recipients <Cite k="tv-free-travel" /> <Tag kind="delivered" />. Cheaper fares are only useful where a service reaches your town, which for a lot of the state is the real problem.

<h2 id="education-and-health" tabindex="-1">Education and health</h2>

Free TAFE began in 2019, before the federal program <Cite k="tafe-history" /> <Tag kind="delivered" />. The government says more than 245,000 students have benefited <Cite k="tafe-245k" /> <Tag kind="govt" />, which is a participation count, not a completion or employment result. Free Kinder began in 2023 and covers funded programs at participating services rather than making childcare free <Cite k={["kinder-launch", "kinder-rules"]} /> <Tag kind="delivered" />. The school building authority counts 121 new schools opened since 2017 <Cite k="vsba" /> <Tag kind="govt" />.

The Victorian Heart Hospital opened in 2023 and the new Footscray Hospital in February 2026 <Cite k={["heart-hospital", "footscray-hospital"]} /> <Tag kind="delivered" />. Bendigo’s hospital opened in 2017, but construction started in June 2014 under the previous government, so that one is shared credit <Cite k="bendigo-hospital" />. Labor wrote nurse-to-patient ratios into law in 2015, protecting arrangements that had lived in enterprise agreements since 2000 <Cite k="ratios-act" /> <Tag kind="delivered" />, and public fertility care has run since 2022 <Cite k="fertility-care" /> <Tag kind="delivered" />. Staffing, waiting times and regional access still need their own scrutiny.

<h2 id="household-support-and-social-reform" tabindex="-1">Household support and social reform</h2>

Renters gained ground: from November 2025 the last no-fault ground for ending a fixed-term lease was removed, alongside a ban on rent bidding <Cite k="rental-changes" /> <Tag kind="delivered" />. Solar Homes has supported hundreds of thousands of installations <Cite k="solar-homes" /> <Tag kind="govt" />, and renewables supplied 42.4% of the state’s electricity generation in the 2024–25 financial year, by the official progress report <Cite k="vret-progress" /> <Tag kind="outcome" />. That’s generation, not all energy use, and households, private investors and federal policy share the credit.

I also give weight to voluntary assisted dying, operating since 2019 after a conscience vote <Cite k="vad" /> <Tag kind="delivered" />; the ban on conversion practices <Cite k="conversion-ban" /> <Tag kind="delivered" />; and the family violence reforms, where the government reported in 2023 that all 227 royal commission recommendations had been implemented <Cite k="rcfv-227" /> <Tag kind="govt" />. Implemented isn’t the same as solved.

Victoria’s statewide Treaty commenced in December 2025 <Cite k="treaty" /> <Tag kind="delivered" />. Credit belongs to the First Peoples’ Assembly and communities as much as to the government. The Liberals have said they’d repeal the Act if elected <Cite k="wilson-treaty" />, so this one is on the ballot as well as in the record.

<h2 id="what-this-doesnt-settle" tabindex="-1">What this doesn’t settle</h2>

A delivery record answers the first of my five questions and leaves the other four open: who bears the cost, what the outcomes are, and whether the alternative is credible. The debit side, the integrity findings sorted by what each body actually found, is the [next article](/articles/victoria-2026-integrity-findings). Crime, debt, hospital waiting times and housing supply are not here yet because I haven’t checked them, and I’d rather say so.
