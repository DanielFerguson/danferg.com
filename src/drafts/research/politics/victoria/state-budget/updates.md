# Part 10: progression, corrections and review

## 15 September 2026 — first draft

- Built around the tension between the $1 billion operating surplus and the four-year cash deficits of more than $30 billion, with the Auditor-General's November 2025 report as the spine.
- Interest ($24.4 million a day; 6.7% of revenue; $10.6 billion by 2028–29) is connected to the regional road maintenance gap from part 2 as the draft's central regional argument, with the comparison tagged as the author's arithmetic.
- The writer's own grievance is corrected in the text: the PBO's 56.6% per-person metropolitan advantage reverses to 22% in the regions' favour once five major projects are excluded, so the grievance is specifically about megaprojects, not about ordinary regional programs.
- Both parties' unshowable numbers are named as such: Labor's $10.8 billion black-hole release publishes no arithmetic; the Coalition's $15 billion Big Build figure traces to an estimate the CFMEU administrator redacted as untested.
- Explicitly fair to the Coalition on costings: "no published costing I could find" is not "never costed", the PBO keeps client costings confidential, and its election commitment tracker is a JavaScript page this research could not read.
- No source located calls the position a solvency crisis; the ratings (AA, Aa2, both stable) are stated alongside S&P's analyst comment about there being no path to repayment.
- The `money` tag was considered and dropped: it is defined for personal financial choices, so the article keeps the three politics tags used across the series.
