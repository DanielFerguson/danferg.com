# The state budget: Coalition fiscal position

Checked **15 September 2026**. Liberal leader and shadow treasurer Jess Wilson; Nationals leader Danny O'Brien; shadow finance Bridget Vallence. Joint items marked **[joint]** and counted once. Party-site access problems are recorded in parts 7 and 8; the primary records here are the 10-year plan release and Hansard.

## The plan

**"Liberals and Nationals unveil 10-year economic plan"**, 8 May 2026, via Mirage News (party release mirror), https://www.miragenews.com/liberals-nationals-unveil-10-year-economic-plan-1670059/ (live) **[joint]**:
- Fiscal targets: a **cash surplus by 2032**; reduce what it calls Victoria's "$1 million per hour" interest bill; retire the $200bn debt.
- Payroll tax: threshold to **$1.2m by 2028–29**; metropolitan rate to **4.8% from 2030–31**.
- Taxes to repeal: emergency services levy, "taxes on schools", "taxes on GP visits", short-stay levy; first-home-buyer stamp duty free threshold to $1m; land-tax threshold progressively to $300,000.
- Savings and institutions named: "Scrapping Labor's divisive Treaty – saving taxpayers $1 billion"; a hiring freeze on back-office roles; a **Charter of Budget Honesty and a real-time expenditure tracker**; a royal commission to end "$15 billion of Big Build corruption"; a construction-sector watchdog.

**Wilson's budget reply**, Hansard, 12 May 2026, https://www.parliament.vic.gov.au/parliamentary-activity/hansard/hansard-details/HANSARD-2145855009-35502 (live; already cited as `wilson-budget-reply-2026`): the stamp-duty and land-tax pledges, savings of "between $500 and $975 per year" for affected land-tax payers, "$534 per year" in household cost-of-living savings, the Treaty $1bn, and a **2% reduction in the total public sector through attrition, not lay-offs**.

**"Our First 100 Days"**, 20 August 2026 (part 7's `nats-100-days`): recall Parliament to abolish the ESVF, the "schools tax" and the "GP tax"; 3,000 police; cancel VNI West; open conventional gas; start on one million potholes; Fair Share Guarantee. No housing, land-tax or budget-process item.

No offset is tied to any specific measure, and **no consolidated Coalition costings document was located**. Newbury's May 2025 pledge — "We will make every announcement fully costed and released before the election" (ABC, 27 May 2025) — has not yet been met by anything this research could read; the PBO's 2026 election commitment tracker is a JavaScript application this tool cannot read, so whether costings have been lodged or published is **unknown**, not "never costed".

## Positions not found

The COVID debt levy beyond the land-tax threshold (the payroll surcharge on large employers, the fixed charges, the 2033 end date); the GST; public sector wages policy; asset sales or privatisation; any response to the cash-deficit series; any figure for what scrapping VNI West costs to exit or saves.

## Gaps and research limits

1. vicliberal.org.au renders poorly and no Liberal policy or costings PDF was located; the Mirage mirror and Hansard are the primary records used.
2. The $1bn Treaty saving is the Coalition's own figure over an unstated period; no independent costing of Treaty implementation was located.
3. The "$15 billion of Big Build corruption" figure traces to the Watson review's estimate, which the administrator redacted as untested (part 5) — it is not an audited saving available to fund commitments.
4. The 2% public sector reduction cannot be converted to dollars or headcount here: VPSC workforce data pages would not render.
5. Whether any Coalition policy has been submitted to the PBO is unknown.
