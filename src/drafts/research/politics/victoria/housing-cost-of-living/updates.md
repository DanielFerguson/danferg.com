# Part 7: progression, corrections and review

## 15 September 2026 — first draft

- Baseline established from official and independent series. Two gaps carried into the draft as stated facts: no 2026 quarter of the Homes Victoria rental report and no 2026 housing-register figure had been published when checked.
- The Regional Housing Fund’s “377 completed” was decomposed (90 new, 72 transitional, 3 First Nations, 187 upgrades) and the draft counts new dwellings “generously, under 200” rather than quoting 377 as new homes.
- The Coalition’s stamp-duty pledge is compared with the existing $750,000 relief threshold (SRO page); the draft poses, rather than answers, how many regional first-home buyers fall in the $750,000–$1 million band, because no figure was found.
- The Greens’ two revenue figures for the same investor land tax ($6.4 billion over ten years in the July release; about $2 billion over four years on the platform page) are both cited and the discrepancy is stated.
- One Nation’s federal planks (immigration, superannuation) are separated from its state-level items.
- Independents are named only where a housing statement was found (McKeeman, Nelson) and the draft states that neither is necessarily on the author’s ballot.
