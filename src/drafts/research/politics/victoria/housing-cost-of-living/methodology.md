# Part 7: research method and traceability

Checked 15 September 2026, Australia/Melbourne. Scope is housing (renting, buying, social and public housing, homelessness, supply and planning) and household cost-of-living measures controlled by the state, for Victoria’s 2026 election. Energy bills and the emergency-services levy are mentioned in one line each and handled in their own parts. Health costs are parked with the healthcare article.

Method follows part 6. Three parallel research passes (baseline and government; Coalition; crossbench) each recorded, per claim: title, publisher, published date, URL, date checked, whether the live page was read in full or only seen indexed, a short quoted passage, the mechanism, gross/new/net funding and period, funding source or offsets, timing, regional distribution, and what remains unspecified. Joint Liberal–National commitments are counted once. Silence in the sources found is recorded as a research limit, not a finding.

Before drafting, the coordinating writer re-fetched the load-bearing pages directly rather than relying on the research passes: the Homes Victoria rental report landing page, the Regional Housing Fund page, Anglicare’s 2026 release, the Property Council’s 2025 completions release, the ABS March 2026 building activity release, the NHSAC August 2026 report, the Victorian Housing Register page, the budget cost-of-living page, the Liberal stamp-duty policy page, Wilson’s 12 May 2026 budget reply, the PBO land-tax paper, the two Greens July releases and the Greens platform page, the Parliament short-stay levy explainer, and the SRO first-home-buyer duty page. Figures that could not be confirmed from a fetched page are not in the draft or are labelled as another body’s reading.

Evidence rules: a target is not a completion; “completed or underway” blends delivered with funded; a budget allocation is not an outcome; a party page proves a proposal and nothing about cost or effect; a procedural vote to allow a bill’s introduction is not support for the bill; PBO client costings can remain confidential, so “no published costing located” never becomes “never costed”. A new `party` source kind was added to the citation system so party documents are labelled as such in the margin notes and source list.

Writing constraints: first person, Dan’s stated position (traditional Labor voter, regional, vote open), no invented personal circumstances; an `AUTHOR:` comment marks where a real local example would help. Same five questions and the same comparison-table columns as part 6. No numerical party score.
