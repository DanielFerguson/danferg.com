# Crossbench, minor parties, independents and community groups: energy and climate, Victoria 2026

Research notes for the energy/climate article. All sources checked 2026-09-15. "Live" = page fetched directly today; "indexed" = seen only via search snippet or a secondary report. Quotes are under 15 words. Where a source is silent, that is a research limit, not a finding.

Source-record format: **Title** — Publisher, published date, URL, live/indexed. Quote. Mechanism / funding / period / unspecified.

---

## (a) Victorian Greens

**Headline finding:** As of 15 September 2026 the Greens have *not* published a fresh 2026 energy platform with costings. Their public material is (i) a standing policy document updated October 2025, (ii) a "100% renewable by 2030" platform page that is undated and substantially recycles 2022 commitments, and (iii) 2026 media releases that attack rivals without new figures. Treat every dollar figure below as a 2022 commitment unless stated otherwise.

1. **Energy Policy** — Australian Greens Victoria, last updated 4 Oct 2025, https://greens.org.au/vic/policies/energy-policy, live. Quote: "no later than 2030" (binding stationary-energy targets). Mechanism: aims/principles document, not costed pledges. Commits to 100% renewable electricity; "no public funding for the existing fossil fuel industry"; public ownership of transmission and distribution; publicly owned pumped hydro and big batteries; community-owned microgrids; government-backed household loans for efficiency, solar and batteries; structural adjustment for regional communities. Unspecified: coal closure dates, dollar amounts, timelines, SEC role, landholder payments.

2. **Tackle the climate crisis (platform page)** — Australian Greens Victoria, undated (content matches Nov 2022 releases), https://greens.org.au/vic/platform/climate, live. Quote: "1 million Victoria homes off gas". Mechanism: 80% emissions cut by 2030, net zero 2035; coal phase-out by 2030 with a "guaranteed job" for coal/gas workers; $10bn public renewables; 3 GW offshore wind by 2030; "Power Victoria" public agency; solar and batteries for public housing; "solar gardens" for renters/apartments; up to $15,000 EV replacement grant; doubling fossil-fuel royalties. Funding: royalties doubling (2022 PBO estimate). Period: six years for the gas plan. Unspecified: whether any of this is re-endorsed for 2026; interaction with the now-legislated SEC.

3. **100% Renewable Victoria** — Australian Greens Victoria, page last modified 7 Mar 2020, https://greens.org.au/vic/renewablevic, live. Quote: "remove the license to operate for all of Victoria's dirty coal plants by 2030". Includes $9bn public large-scale renewables, $500m storage, $300m Latrobe Valley battery, $10m community energy hubs, solar on all stand-alone public housing and all public schools, public ownership of upgraded grid sections. Stale page (2018-era targets like 50% by 2025); use only as evidence of continuity.

4. **PBO post-election costings (2022)** — Victorian Parliamentary Budget Office, Nov 2022 baseline, https://static.pbo.vic.gov.au/files/PBO-POST-Greens-Costings-FINAL.pdf, live. Quote: "Getting Off Gas ... $2,190.0 million" (forward estimates). Renewable energy and storage $10.0bn to 30 June 2030; coal royalty/job guarantee net -$1,309.1m extended period; Latrobe Valley Authority $480m to 2035. These are the only independent costings of Greens energy policy found; no 2026 PBO costing exists yet.

5. **Greens announce top climate priorities (2022)** — Australian Greens Victoria, 13 Nov 2022, https://greens.org.au/vic/news/media-release/greens-announce-top-climate-priorities-including-coal-workers-job-guarantee, live. Quote: "the only party this election to present a comprehensive costed plan". Confirms the $6,000-per-household gas appliance grant (1 million households) and coal closure legislated for 2030. 2022, not 2026.

6. **Greens launch plan for renters' solar (2022)** — Australian Greens Victoria, 22 Nov 2022, https://greens.org.au/vic/news/media-release/greens-launch-game-changing-plan-give-renters-and-apartment-dwellers-access, live. Quote: "Solar shouldn't just be for homeowners." Mechanism: $1,000 grants toward panels in shared "solar gardens", bill credits portable when tenant moves; funded within the $10bn envelope. 2022 commitment; no 2026 restatement found.

7. **SEC constitutional amendments** — Australian Greens Victoria, 3 Apr 2024, https://greens.org.au/vic/news/media-release/greens-support-sec-after-securing-key-wins-increase-renewables-lower-power, live; and The Australian Greens, 13 Sep 2024, https://greens.org.au/news/media-release/greens-reforms-enshrine-renewables-constitution-pass-parliament, live. Quote: "a publicly owned SEC will drive investment in renewables and lower power prices". Mechanism: Greens amendments to the Constitution Amendment (SEC) Bill 2023 bar dividends from SEC capital/profits into general revenue and won a public commitment against a financial accommodation levy. This is the concrete Greens "SEC expansion" record; there is no published 2026 SEC funding figure from the Greens.

8. **Only the Greens will stop Victoria going backwards on climate** — Australian Greens Victoria, 31 Aug 2026, https://greens.org.au/vic/news/media-release/only-greens-will-stop-victoria-going-backwards-climate-change, live. Quote (Sandell): "the only ones with real policies to build more renewables". Mechanism: positioning statement — end coal and gas extraction, expand public renewables, "ensure regional communities benefit". No figures, dates, or mechanism for regional benefit stated.

9. **Greens 2026 media release index** — Australian Greens Victoria, Aug–Sep 2026, https://greens.org.au/vic/news, live. Releases of 25 Aug (AEMO/data centres), 31 Aug, 2 Sep (record warmth; climate and housing), 11 Sep (data centre compensation for residents). None announces a costed energy commitment. Research limit: the "record warmth" release URL 404s.

10. **Election Tracker Greens profile** — electiontracker.au, verified 2 Aug 2026, https://electiontracker.au/elections/vic/2026/parties/greens/policies, live. Lists the Oct 2025 policy document and a 16 Jan 2026 release calling to "immediately ban all new coal and gas projects" and set up a climate-resilience fund financed partly by coal/gas companies; all marked cost "unspecified".

11. **Transition Away from Coal Bill 2023 (context)** — Australian Greens Victoria, 21 Jun 2023, https://greens.org.au/vic/news/media-release/bill-set-end-date-coal-victoria-knocked-down-labor, live. Greens private member's bill for a 2030 coal end-date; Labor declined to debate it. Shows the 2030 date is a standing Greens position.

**Greens: what is missing.** No 2026 numbers for SEC capital, household electrification subsidies, renter/public-housing solar, transmission community benefit, or offshore wind. No stated position on VNI West route or undergrounding beyond "public ownership of transmission" (policy doc) and the VicGrid amendments in (e).

## (b) One Nation Victoria

12. **Affordable Energy Policy** — One Nation Victoria, undated, https://vic.onenation.org.au/energy-policy, live. Quote: "Phase out new taxpayer subsidies for renewable projects". Mechanism: no new renewable subsidies (existing contracts honoured); change NEM rules so coal is not disadvantaged; gas — 200 PJ/yr local extraction plus 200 PJ interstate reserve before export, reverse ban on gas connections in new homes, continue offshore exploration; nuclear — back federal ban repeal, pursue SMRs; "urgent review of above-ground transmission lines" with preference to reroute or underground; prohibit offshore wind; ban wind/solar on prime agricultural land; rehabilitation bonds; de-privatise transmission/distribution into a public not-for-profit entity; CCS; 90-day fuel storage. Funding/period: none stated. Unspecified: cost of undergrounding, which projects, who pays, timeline.

13. **Election Tracker One Nation profile** — electiontracker.au, verified 2 Aug 2026, https://electiontracker.au/elections/vic/2026/parties/one-nation/policies, live. Quote: "Reliability and affordability should determine investment". Also records opposition to net zero by 2050 (vic.onenation.org.au/our-environment) and a cost-of-living page pairing "end taxpayer subsidies for wind/solar" with removing insurance duty. All uncosted.

14. **One Nation commits to coal at Loy Yang and lifting nuclear ban** — ABC News, 31 Aug 2026, https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-08-31/one-nation-victoria-energy-policy-announcement/107097686, live. Quote (Pickering): "Those figures haven't been discussed just yet." Launch at Loy Yang A with Hanson and state leader Warren Pickering: keep Loy Yang A open beyond 2035; repeal the 1983 Nuclear Activities (Prohibitions) Act; abolish REZs in the west and offshore wind zones; abandon net zero. AGL restated 2035 closure. Grattan's Tony Wood warned of reliability/price risk; Bruce Mountain called REZ abolition a rebrand.

15. **One Nation promises new coal plants** — RenewEconomy (Callum Godde/AAP), 31 Aug 2026, https://reneweconomy.com.au/one-nation-turns-up-heat-with-firey-energy-pitch-promising-new-coal-and-old-and-nuclear/, live. Quote: "Loy Yang will not be closing in 2035." Yallourn (2028) conceded as possibly "too far gone". Funding: industry to self-fund capex; no figures. Resolve poll cited: One Nation 23% primary in Victoria.

16. **One Nation energy preview in Marnoo** — ABC News, 30 Aug 2026, https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-08-30/one-nation-energy-policy-vni-west/107094846, live. Quote (Hanson): "look at nuclear as well." Commitments: remove VicGrid compulsory access/acquisition powers on VNI West; abolish the regional REZ; oppose large-scale renewables on farmland. Declined to say how existing contracts unwind. Regional candidates named: Donna Buxton (Ripon), Stuart Robinson (Wendouree), Paul Ballinger (Eureka), Vaughn Williams (Western Victoria Region).

17. **One Nation draws large crowd at Marnoo** — The Weekly Advertiser, 2 Sep 2026, https://theweeklyadvertiser.com.au/articles/one-nation-draws-large-crowd-at-marnoo/, live. Quote (Hanson): "you will not be paying your fines. That will all be wiped." Balance-of-power pledge to wipe VicGrid access fines. Buxton's own statement listed cost of living, health, debt and "impact of government policy on farmers" — not a specific VNI West quote from her.

18. **Farmers fight back in Marnoo** — Australian Rural & Regional News, 3 Sep 2026, https://arr.news/2026/09/03/farmers-fight-back-in-marnoo/, live. Quote (Pickering): "We do not have an energy problem ... a policy problem." Crowd ~1,000. Note: research limit — no One Nation candidate quote on wind farms in a named regional seat other than the Marnoo/Ripon material above; One Nation had not named candidates for Benambra or Mildura in the sources found.

## (c) Other minor parties (one paragraph each, only where found)

**Libertarian Party.** *Dump Net Zero* — Libertarians Victoria, undated, https://www.vic.libertarians.org.au/dump_net_zero, live. Quote: "Make Net-Zero Voluntary, Not Mandatory." All technologies "including coal, gas ... and nuclear"; "cut the $1 billion corporate energy welfare program"; remove nuclear prohibition. No transmission position stated. Limbrick's votes: see (e).

**Family First.** *Family First fights for choice on gas* — Family First Party Australia, 25 Jun 2025, https://www.familyfirstparty.org.au/family_first_fights_for_choice_on_gas_all_the_way_to_the_victorian_and_nsw_elections, live. Quote: "a rational energy transition: from coal to gas to nuclear". Opposes Victoria's gas hot-water and new-home connection restrictions. No Victorian candidates or transmission position in the source; no costings.

**Victorian Socialists.** *Policies* — victoriansocialists.org.au, 2026 platform, https://www.victoriansocialists.org.au/policies, live. Quote: "100 percent publicly owned renewable energy"; a "public takeover" to cap electricity and gas bills. No dates, targets, or transmission position.

**Animal Justice Party.** *Energy* — AJP national, undated, https://www.animaljusticeparty.org/energy, live. Quote: "rapidly transition to a carbon free energy infrastructure" (15–20 years). Rejects coal and gas; prefers solar; opposes new hydro. No Victorian 2026 statement found; Purcell's votes in (e).

**Shooters, Fishers and Farmers; Legalise Cannabis.** No published 2026 Victorian energy or transmission policy found on electiontracker.au (party pages 404 on policy subpages) or via search. Their parliamentary votes appear in (e). Research limit, not a finding.

## (e) Key parliamentary votes 2024–26 (Legislative Council unless stated)

30. **Climate Change and Energy Legislation Amendment (Renewable Energy and Storage Targets) Bill 2023** — Parliament of Victoria news, 14 Mar 2024, https://www.parliament.vic.gov.au/news/infrastructure/energy-targets/, live; Council debate 7 Mar 2024, https://www.parliament.vic.gov.au/parliamentary-activity/hansard/hansard-details/HANSARD-974425065-25687, live. Legislated 95% renewables by 2035, 2035 interim emissions target 75–80%, offshore wind and storage targets. Greens (Mansfield) supported with an amendment that passed and sought 100% by 2030; Libertarian Limbrick "absolutely" opposed; One Nation's Tyrrell said she would not support it, citing farmland (a "70%" coverage claim) and wildlife. Coalition did not oppose but wanted gas retained. Division tallies not in the pages fetched.

31. **Energy and Public Land Legislation Amendment (Enabling Offshore Wind Energy) Bill 2024** — Parliament news, 10 May 2024, https://www.parliament.vic.gov.au/news/infrastructure/offshore-wind/, live; Council debate 30 Apr 2024, https://www.parliament.vic.gov.au/parliamentary-activity/hansard/hansard-details/HANSARD-974425065-26202, live. Passed; Greens supported with environmental-protection concerns; Coalition moved "fit and proper person" and consultation amendments; Nationals' Bath supported. Division names not extracted.

32. **Motion to revoke planning amendment VC261 (renewable facility appeal rights)** — Council Hansard, 1 May 2024, https://www.parliament.vic.gov.au/parliamentary-activity/hansard/hansard-details/HANSARD-974425065-26283, live. Davis motion negatived 17–20. Greens (Ratnam) voted No; Limbrick (Libertarian), Tyrrell (One Nation) and Deeming (independent) voted Aye with the Coalition. Inference only: the 20 Noes imply Legalise Cannabis and AJP voted with government; verify against the Council minutes.

33. **National Electricity (Victoria) Amendment (VicGrid) Bill 2024** — Council Hansard, 2 May 2024, https://www.parliament.vic.gov.au/parliamentary-activity/hansard/hansard-details/HANSARD-974425065-26372, live. Three committee divisions, each 20–16 (one 20–12). Greens (Copsey, Mansfield, Ratnam), Legalise Cannabis (Ettershank, Payne) and AJP (Purcell) voted with Labor; Limbrick, Tyrrell, Deeming, Heath and Somyurek voted with the Coalition (including on a Coalition community-advisory-committee amendment and cost-recovery clause). Quote: "Amendments negatived."

34. **National Electricity (Victoria) Amendment (VicGrid Stage 2 Reform) Bill 2025** — legislation.vic.gov.au bill page, https://www.legislation.vic.gov.au/bills/national-electricity-victoria-amendment-vicgrid-stage-2-reform-bill-2025, live: Council second and third reading 28 Aug 2025; assent 16 Sep 2025 (Act 38/2025); amendments agreed from Legalise Cannabis and Greens (1 and 2) and Opposition (4–6); One Nation amendments circulated, not agreed. Council debate, 28 Aug 2025, https://www.parliament.vic.gov.au/parliamentary-activity/hansard/hansard-details/HANSARD-974425065-32191, live: Greens (Mansfield) supported with amendments expanding the community energy fund to biodiversity and removing the Treasurer's power to sweep it into consolidated revenue; Tyrrell circulated amendments removing forced-access powers and $12,000+ fines; Limbrick opposed on net-zero grounds; Labor cited $200,000/km over 25 years to landholders plus $8,000/km²/yr. ABC Rural, 29 Aug 2025, https://www.abc.net.au/news/rural/2025-08-29/vic-grid-legislation-passes-victorian-parliament-renewable/105710780, live: passed late-night with Legalise Cannabis and AJP; fine halved from $12,200 but "reasonable force" retained; VFF's Brett Hosking: "It's not fair. It's not just." Latrobe Valley Express, 2 Sep 2025, https://latrobevalleyexpress.com.au/news/2025/09/02/vicgrid-bill-passes-with-amendments/, live: fine cut to $8,000; Mansfield: "recognising the essential role our communities are playing". Third-reading division names not obtained (daily Hansard PDF blocked by robots.txt).

- **One Nation:** no costings anywhere; no candidate-level VNI West or wind-farm quotes beyond the Marnoo/Ripon event; the undergrounding "review" has no scope, cost or timeline.

- **Community groups:** MCHPA's current asks were not retrievable; the VFF's own release on the scrap pledge 404s; no VFF setback-distance figure was found.

Party labels in (32) were corrected from a mislabelled extraction and should be verified against the minutes.
