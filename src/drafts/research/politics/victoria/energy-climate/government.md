Research notes for the 28 November 2026 election. Sources checked 2026-09-15. "Live" = fetched and read; "indexed" = search result only. Status labels: **promise / funded / delivered / measured outcome / government figure / independent finding**.

## 1. Transmission — the big Carroll change

**Western Renewables Link paused (15 Sept 2026).** ACM wire via Border Mail (live) https://www.bordermail.com.au/story/9350906/major-transmission-project-paused-after-public-outcry/. Quote (Carroll): "Victorian consumers deserve updated, transparent costings." Independent cost assessment to be published by end-2026; no compulsory acquisitions meanwhile; cost cited $479m (2019) → $1.53bn (PwC 2024); the line crosses Labor-held Melton, Ripon and Eureka. **Government decision; delivered as a pause. Effect: no decision before the election.** Establishes a clear break from the Allan/D'Ambrosio line ("If we want to keep the lights on, we need to build transmission" — D'Ambrosio, ABC 1 Aug 2025, live https://www.abc.net.au/news/2025-08-01/transmission-line-vni-west-cost-blow-out-victoria-farmers-fight/105599880). Does not establish cancellation, nor what happens to VNI West, which connects at Bulgana to WRL.

**VNI West.** No Carroll statement found. Standing position: proceeding via Transmission Company Victoria, completion late 2030 (RenewEconomy 1 July 2025, live https://reneweconomy.com.au/key-transmission-link-delayed-by-two-years-in-new-blow-to-wind-and-solar-projects/). **Status under Carroll: not verified.**

**Benefit-sharing.** Landholder payments $8,000/km/yr for 25 years (Premier's office 23 Feb 2023, live https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/landholder-payments-fairer-renewables-transition). Six REZs declared 3 June 2026 under VicGrid (live https://www.vicgrid.com.au/about/news/news-stories/renewable-energy-zones-declared). Neighbour payments and REZ community energy fund amounts — **not verified**; no 2026 expansion announcement found.

## 2. Offshore wind

- Promise: 2 GW by 2032, 4 GW 2035, 9 GW 2040 (legislated). D'Ambrosio 27 Jan 2026 committed the first auction to August 2026 (live https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/victorias-first-offshore-wind-auction-go-live-august): "Victoria will host the country's first offshore wind industry"; "over 6,000 jobs".
- Symes 7 Aug 2026: auction "going ahead this month as planned" (RenewEconomy, live). **Delivered: Request for Proposal opened 26 Aug 2026** for 2 GW; closes Aug 2027; contracts 2028; ESEM contract structure; claim of 68,000 transition jobs by 2040, 37% regional (Premier's office 26 Aug 2026, live https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/offshore-wind-powering-ahead). Quote: "a giant leap towards getting Australia's first offshore wind projects built."
- Independent check: VAGO (3 Dec 2025, live https://www.audit.vic.gov.au/report/managing-transition-renewable-energy) — 2032 target will be missed; no port settled. ABC 21 May 2026 (live): Star of the South first power 2032–2037.
- **Net: auction is real; 2032 target is dead on the auditor's reading; government has not formally moved the target.**

## 3. SEC

- Promise (2022 election, restated): $1bn, 4.5 GW, publicly owned. Delivered to date: Melbourne Renewable Energy Hub (600 MW battery, SEC $245m stake) operating Dec 2025; Horsham solar connected Sept 2026 with battery to follow; Delburn wind under construction; Easy Electric SEC household service. SEC news (live) https://www.secvictoria.com.au/news; premier release on Horsham (indexed) https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/sec-renewable-energy-park-powering-ahead-horsham. **Government figures; partly delivered.**
- Symes now holds the SEC portfolio; no new SEC pipeline announcement under Carroll found. Whether the SEC becomes a retailer (VCOSS ask) — no commitment found.

## 4. VRET and emissions

- Standing: 65% by 2030, 95% by 2035; 75–80% cut by 2035; net zero 2045 — all legislated, no change under Carroll found.
- Government claims: 42.4% renewables in 2024/25, 2025 target beaten (energy.vic.gov.au news, indexed https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/about-energy/news/news-stories/Victoria-powers-beyond-its-2025-renewable-energy-target). **Government figure, corroborated by tabled report.**
- Government response to Coalition's 13–14 Aug 2026 "let Loy Yang A and B run to end of life" pledge: renewables are "the most cost-effective way" to replace coal; AEMO and VicGrid "have prepared for Yallourn's closure since 2021" (ABC, live https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-08-14/victorian-opposition-coal-power-pledge/107033138). No Labor move to extend Yallourn found; Prof Mountain notes the 2021 agreement leaves that door open.

## 5. Gas policy — has Carroll softened it?

- **Finding: no evidence, as of 15 Sept 2026, that Carroll or Symes has paused, deferred or repealed the gas-electrification rules.** The current energy.vic.gov.au standards page (live https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/households/save-with-all-electric-home/electric-and-efficiency-standards-for-buildings) still lists 1 Jan 2027 (new builds all-electric) and 1 March 2027 (hot-water replacement; rental standards). Searches for "Carroll gas ban paused/review" return only the WRL pause story.
- **The softening happened under Allan, June 2025**: start pushed from March 2026 to March 2027; owner-occupier gas heating replacement mandate dropped; repairs allowed; cost/space exemptions; cooktops never included (Geelong Times, live https://timesnewsgroup.com.au/geelongtimes/real-estate/home-gas-appliance-replacement-bans-eased-pushed-back/; Premier's office 24 June 2025, live https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/securing-victorias-gas-supply-while-slashing-energy-bills). Quote (Allan): "we are reserving it for industry."
- Supply side under Labor 2026: Annie gas field and Golden Beach storage approved 7 May 2026 (live https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/more-gas-flow-victoria): "Only Labor has a plan to manage the energy transition responsibly". Gas Security Statement claims 12 PJ/yr unlocked by 2029, 44 PJ by 2035 (June 2025 release). **Government figures; approvals delivered, gas not yet flowing.**
- Banksia note that Symes was handed "sensitive issues in the lead up to the election including gas" is the only signal of possible movement — **speculative**.
