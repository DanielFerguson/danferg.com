# Energy, climate and regional Victoria — baseline (official / independent data)

Research notes for the 28 November 2026 Victorian election. All sources checked 2026-09-15. "Live" = page fetched and read; "indexed" = seen only in search results. Status labels: **promise / funded / delivered / measured outcome / government figure / independent finding**.

## 1. Household bills

**Victorian Default Offer 2026–27 — final decision.** Essential Services Commission, 20 May 2026. https://www.esc.vic.gov.au/electricity-and-gas/prices-tariffs-and-benchmarks/victorian-default-offer/victorian-default-offer-price-review-2026-27 (live). Quote: "five per cent lower for domestic customers and six per cent lower for small business". Typical residential flat-rate bill (4,000 kWh) $1,591 from 1 July 2026, down $84 (5%) from $1,675; small business (10,000 kWh) $3,380, down $240 (6%). Driver: lower environmental, wholesale and network costs. **Independent regulator finding / measured price.** Establishes: the regulated standing-offer benchmark fell in 2026–27. Does not establish what any household actually pays (most are on market offers) or gas bill movements. Final decision PDF: https://www.esc.vic.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/Victorian%20Default%20Offer%202026%E2%80%9327%20Final%20Decision%20Paper.pdf (indexed).

**Gas prices.** The ESC does not set a gas default offer; gas retail prices are monitored, not capped. The last ESC media release with a gas figure I could read was the 2021–22 market report (29 Sept 2022): residential gas offers +19% in a year. https://www.esc.vic.gov.au/media-centre/new-energy-report-tracks-victorian-retail-response-volatile-wholesale-prices (live). Typical 2026 Victorian gas bill: **not verified**.

**Hardship and disconnections — ESC Victorian Energy Market Report 2023–24 (annual).** ESC, 29 Nov 2024. PDF https://www.esc.vic.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/RPT%20-%20Victorian%20Energy%20Market%20Report%20-%20Annual%202024.pdf (live); media release https://www.esc.vic.gov.au/media-centre/annual-victorian-energy-market-report-shows-more-customers-debt-fewer-disconnections (live). Quote (Chair Gerard Brody): "many customers in arrears that are not receiving payment assistance". Figures (2023–24): electricity customers in arrears >$300, 121,000 monthly average (5%), up 20,000; gas 93,000 (5%), up 14,000; average arrears $1,294 electricity / $1,306 gas; disconnections for non-payment 7,669 electricity (−31%) and 3,474 gas (−18%); tailored-assistance customers ~69,500 electricity / ~62,800 gas; 38% of those unable to afford ongoing usage. **Independent finding / measured outcome.** Establishes: debt rising while disconnections fell. Does not establish 2024–25 or 2025–26 figures — a newer annual report was not found in search; **2024–25 numbers not verified**.

**Energy concession.** DFFH, annual electricity concession page https://services.dffh.vic.gov.au/annual-electricity-concession (live). Quote: "17.5 per cent of electricity usage and service costs". Applies after retailer discounts/solar credits; excludes first $171.60 of the annual bill; bills over $3,895.13/yr (from 1 Dec 2024) need the separate Excess Energy Concession. Eligibility: Pensioner Concession, Health Care, DVA Gold cards. **Government program, in force.**

**Power Saving Bonus — final round and closure.** Premier's office, 27 Jan 2026, https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/real-power-bill-relief-victorians (live). $100 payment for concession-card holders only; applications closed midnight 31 March 2026; 245,000+ households / $24.5m paid since August 2025; program lifetime 4.5m applications and "over $1 billion" since 2018. **Government figure; delivered (round closed).** Establishes: the universal $250 bonus era is over; the last round was concession-targeted and is now closed. Does not establish any replacement for 2026–27.

**Solar Homes.** Solar Victoria, 29 May 2026 (updated 30 June 2026), https://www.solar.vic.gov.au/solar-homes-program-continuing-provide-targeted-assistance (live). Quote: "over 850,000 small-scale solar PV systems" installed in Victoria; "more than 45,000" battery installs; solar on ~30% of suitable homes vs 14% in 2018; rebates unchanged (PV up to $1,400; hot water up to $1,400 locally made / $1,000 other); 27,000 extra hot-water rebates in 2026–27; household income cap cut from $210,000 to $150,000 from 1 July 2026. **Government figure.** Caution: the 850,000 is all Victorian rooftop PV, not only Solar Homes rebates — the program-specific rebate count is **not verified** here. Budget line (VCOSS reading of 2026–27 Budget): $28.1m for hot-water rebates in 2026–27 — https://vcoss.org.au/vicbudget/2026-victorian-budget/ (live; secondary).

## 2. Renewables share and targets

**VRET Progress Report 2024/25.** DEECA, tabled Oct 2025, https://www.parliament.vic.gov.au/4aaef0/globalassets/tabled-paper-documents/tabled-paper-9503/vret-progress-report-2024-25-final.pdf (live). Quote: renewables were "42.4% of electricity generation in 2024/25", up from 37.8%. Breakdown: wind 21.8%, rooftop solar 10.8%, hydro 4.9%, large solar 4.1%. Installed renewable capacity 14,135 MW at 30 June 2025 (+1,395 MW in year). Storage: 773 MW commissioned, 2.3 GW under construction. Electricity-sector emissions 60.3 Mt (2014/15) → 37.3 Mt (2024/25). Legislated targets: 40% 2025, 65% 2030, 95% 2035; storage 2.6 GW 2030 / 6.3 GW 2035; offshore wind 2 GW 2032, 4 GW 2035, 9 GW 2040. **Government figure (measured outcome for the share).** Establishes: 2025 target met a year early. Does not establish the 2030 trajectory.

**Auditor-General, "Managing the Transition to Renewable Energy".** VAGO, tabled 3 Dec 2025, https://www.audit.vic.gov.au/report/managing-transition-renewable-energy (live). Quote: "Victoria would be more likely to face electricity shortfalls after Yallourn … closes in mid-2028" if risks are not managed. Findings: 2025 target on track; 2030 (65%) depends on full Capacity Investment Scheme allocation plus state support and on-time transmission; 2035 "more difficult"; Victoria **will not meet 2 GW offshore wind by 2032** (optimistic case end-2033); WRL slipped to 2029 (from 2025), VNI West to late 2030 (from 2027–28); SEC on track for 1 GW storage by 2028. **Independent finding.**

## 3. Emissions

## 5. Reliability (AEMO)

**2026 ESOO.** AEMO, 25 Aug 2026, https://www.aemo.com.au/newsroom/media-release/2026-ESOO (live); full report https://www.aemo.com.au/-/media/files/electricity/nem/planning_and_forecasting/nem_esoo/2026/2026-electricity-statement-of-opportunities.pdf (indexed). ## 6. Gas supply

**2026 Gas Statement of Opportunities.** AEMO, 26 Mar 2026, https://www.aemo.com.au/newsroom/media-release/2026-gsoo (live); PDF (indexed) https://www.aemo.com.au/-/media/files/gas/national_planning_and_forecasting/gsoo/2026/2026-gas-statement-of-opportunities.pdf. Quote (Falcon): projects "if committed, may delay forecast shortfalls". Southern peak-day risk now from 2030 (one year later than 2025 GSOO); annual shortfalls from 2030 under most weather; Victorian production −52.8% and daily capacity −35.2% by 2030, consumption −10.5%. RenewEconomy 25 Mar 2026 (live) https://reneweconomy.com.au/gas-shortage-warnings-pushed-out-again-as-big-battery-boom-and-electrification-take-fresh-bite-from-demand/ reads the same report as peak-day risk "by 2029" — the two readings differ by a year; treat the exact first-risk year as 2029–30. **Independent finding.**

**New supply approvals.** Premier's office, 7 May 2026, https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/more-gas-flow-victoria (live): Amplitude Energy's Annie field approved ("over one-third of Victoria's annual gas consumption" from 2028); Golden Beach storage ($1bn, ~18.75 PJ, +70% storage). **Government figure; approved, not yet delivered.**

## 7. Gas policy — what is actually in force

Source: energy.vic.gov.au standards page https://www.energy.vic.gov.au/households/save-with-all-electric-home/electric-and-efficiency-standards-for-buildings (live); Premier's office 24 June 2025 https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/securing-victorias-gas-supply-while-slashing-energy-bills (live); Geelong Times (live) https://timesnewsgroup.com.au/geelongtimes/real-estate/home-gas-appliance-replacement-bans-eased-pushed-back/.
- In force since 1 Jan 2024: no new gas connections for new homes needing a planning permit (Gas Substitution Roadmap; energy.vic.gov.au roadmap page indexed).
- From **1 Jan 2027**: all new homes and new commercial buildings all-electric (industrial/agricultural excluded).
- From **1 March 2027**: failed gas hot-water must be replaced electric (owner-occupiers); rentals must replace failed heating/hot-water with heat pumps / reverse-cycle; rental minimum standards on new leases (cooling, 4-star showerheads, R5.0 ceiling insulation); draught-proofing from 1 July 2027; all rentals efficient cooling by 1 July 2030.
- **Deferred/eased (June 2025, under Allan):** start moved from March 2026 to March 2027; owner-occupier gas *heating* end-of-life ban dropped; repair allowed; cost/space exemptions; gas cooktops never covered.
- Government savings claims: $330/yr hot water ($520 with solar); $880/yr new all-electric home. **Government figures, not independently verified.**
- No change to these dates found under Premier Carroll as of 15 Sept 2026 (see government.md).

## 8. Transmission

- **Western Renewables Link (190 km Bulgana–Sydenham).** Paused 15 Sept 2026 by Premier Carroll pending an independent cost assessment due by end-2026; cost estimate rose from $479m (2019) to $1.53bn (PwC, 2024); no compulsory acquisition during review. ACM/Border Mail https://www.bordermail.com.au/story/9350906/major-transmission-project-paused-after-public-outcry/ (live). VAGO had already noted completion slipped to 2029. **Government decision (today); media report.**
- **VNI West (475 km, Bulgana–Kerang–Dinawan NSW).** AEMO program page (live) https://aemo.com.au/initiatives/major-programs/vni-west: 1.93 GW export / 1.67 GW import, unlocks ~3.4 GW. Delay to late 2030 announced 1 July 2025 (RenewEconomy, live) https://reneweconomy.com.au/key-transmission-link-delayed-by-two-years-in-new-blow-to-wind-and-solar-projects/. Cost: ABC 1 Aug 2025 (live) https://www.abc.net.au/news/2025-08-01/transmission-line-vni-west-cost-blow-out-victoria-farmers-fight/105599880 — "$3.9 billion" to "$7.6 billion", possible $11.4bn; VFF's Brett Hosking: "It's a massive increase and we're not at the end of it." Nexa Advisory (Gannawarra Times, 14 Sept 2026, paywalled, partial) claims a four-year delay to VNI West + WRL adds $33bn to generation costs by 2050 — **consultancy modelling, not verified**.
- **Landholder payments.** $8,000/km/year for 25 years on WRL, VNI West, Marinus and REZ links. Premier's office 23 Feb 2023 https://www.premier.vic.gov.au/landholder-payments-fairer-renewables-transition (live). **Policy in place; uptake not verified.**
- **VicGrid Victorian Transmission Plan 2025 and REZs.** Plan page (live) https://www.vicgrid.com.au/transmission-planning/victorian-transmission-plan — seven candidate zones; 15-year outlook, next plan 2027. Six REZs formally declared 3 June 2026 (South West, Central Highlands, Gippsland, Western, North West, Gippsland Shoreline offshore-cable zone); Central North still under consultation; "much less than one per cent of Victoria's total land area" — https://www.vicgrid.com.au/about/news/news-stories/renewable-energy-zones-declared (live). REZ community fund amounts **not verified**.

## 12. Federal vs state

Federal: Capacity Investment Scheme underwriting (VAGO says Victoria's 2030 target depends on its full allocation; DCCEEW tender pages indexed https://www.dcceew.gov.au/energy/renewable/capacity-investment-scheme); ESEM contract mechanism for offshore wind (ABC, live); national 82% renewables 2030 target; federal energy bill rebates and offshore licensing (feasibility licences are Commonwealth). State: VRET, VDO (ESC), concessions, Solar Homes, SEC, gas building rules, VicGrid REZs, transmission (VNI West via AEMO/TCV; WRL via AusNet under national rules).
