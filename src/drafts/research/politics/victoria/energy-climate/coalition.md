# Coalition (Liberals + Nationals) — energy, climate and regional positions, 2025–26

Research note for the Victorian election (28 Nov 2026). All sources checked 2026-09-15. "Read live" = page fetched and read on that date; "indexed" = only search snippet/summary available. Where the Liberals and Nationals announced together the item is marked **[joint]** and counted once. Party sites carry little detail: vicliberal.org.au home page renders only navigation (read live, no policy pages found); vic.nationals.org.au/policies/ carries a one-line-per-item summary (read live). Most mechanism detail comes from media releases republished via Mirage News/National Tribune and from media reporting (marked *media*).

## 1. Gas: new-home connection ban and electrification rules [joint]

- **Commitment:** "scrapping Labor's ideological gas ban" for new homes; opposes the March 2027 rule that gas hot-water in existing buildings be replaced with electric. Quote: "restore energy choice and ease cost of living pressures" (David Davis).
- Source: "Liberals and Nationals to scrap Labor's gas ban", National Tribune (republished release), 21 Jul 2026, https://www.nationaltribune.com.au/liberals-and-nationals-to-scrap-labor-s-gas-ban/ — read live.
- Corroboration (*media*): The Standard (ACM), "Jess Wilson plans to 'throw open the door' for gas exploration", Katrina Lovell, 20 Aug 2026, https://www.standard.net.au/story/9334083/ — read live. Confirms "overturn the ban on gas in new housing estates"; the "by Christmas" deadline in the headline attaches to the emergency services tax, not the gas ban.
- Mechanism: repeal/regulation change (gas ban was made by planning/regulation, so likely disallowance or amendment); not stated. Funding: nil stated. Timing: not specified beyond "a Wilson Government". Offsets: none. Regional distribution: state-wide. **Unspecified:** whether the ban repeal also covers the Gas Substitution Roadmap rebate programs, hot-water rule reversal timing, and any cost to the state.

## 2. New gas supply: 60-day RFP, onshore exploration, fracking [joint]

- **Commitment:** "launch a statewide Request for Proposal process" within 60 days of forming government inviting producers to nominate conventional gas exploration/production projects; criticises Labor for releasing only "two small exploration areas" and issuing no new onshore conventional permit in a decade. Quote: "More local gas means lower prices, greater energy security" (Wilson).
- Source: "Boosting Local Gas, Lowering Energy Bills Sooner", Mirage News (republished release), 12 Jul 2026, https://www.miragenews.com/boosting-local-gas-lowering-energy-bills-sooner-1708588/ — read live. Nationals summary: https://vic.nationals.org.au/policies/ — read live.
- **Fracking:** Liberal spokesperson: "We do not support fracking"; proposals "considered in the usual way". Source (*media*): Bellarine Times, "Liberals rule out fracking, back gas exploration", 26 Jul 2026, https://timesnewsgroup.com.au/bellarinetimes/news/liberals-rule-out-fracking-back-gas-exploration/ — read live. So the constitutionally entrenched fracking ban (s.97 Constitution Act, 2021) is **not** proposed for repeal; the policy is conventional onshore + offshore only. Symes asked publicly whether they would "overturn the ban on fracking" (ABC, 19 Aug 2026) — the answer on record is no.
- 100-day plan wording: "throw open the doors to new conventional gas" (see §11).
- Mechanism: RFP + "cutting the red tape"; unclear whether new acreage releases under the Petroleum Act or a different permitting track. Funding: nil stated. Timing: RFP inside 60 days; first production timing not stated. Offsets: none. Regional distribution: Otway/Gippsland basins implied, not named in policy. **Unspecified:** acreage, royalty terms, domestic reservation, how gas found in 2027 helps a 2028 Yallourn gap (Davis has acknowledged the 10+ year discovery-to-market problem — RenewEconomy "go after gas" piece, page dateline 15 Sep 2026 but content pre-dates the Aug scrap pledge; date not verified), and whether onshore exploration in the two existing blocks is expanded. Note: Symes says "no restriction on bringing online new gas supply through conventional means" and the Sept 2025 offshore petroleum tender drew no suitable applications (RenewEconomy, 19 Aug 2026).

## 3. VNI West and Western Renewables Link [joint]

- **Evolution:** 7 May 2026 — pause both while a "full review of the Victorian Transmission Plan" is undertaken (Nationals release, "Driving down energy costs and protecting farmer rights", 7 May 2026, https://vic.nationals.org.au/media-releases/danny-obrien-mp-driving-down-energy-costs-and-protecting-farmer-rights/ — read live; ABC, 7 May 2026, https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-05-07/victoria-election-coalition-vows-to-pause-transmission-projects/106651434 — read live). 19 Aug 2026 — **scrap VNI West** outright; WRL "assessed for value for money"/review only. Kealy: "VNI West is dead, buried and cremated". Wilson: "Victoria doesn't need more unnecessary poles and wires".
- Sources (*media*): ABC, "Victorian Coalition promises to scrap VNI West transmission project", 19 Aug 2026, https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-08-19/victorian-coalition-promises-to-scrap-vni-west-transmission-line/107053638 — read live. RenewEconomy, Rachel Williamson, 19 Aug 2026, https://reneweconomy.com.au/we-need-more-gas-victoria-coalition-light-on-detail-after-vowing-to-scrap-vni-west-transmission-link/ — read live. The Energy, Charis Palmer, 19 Aug 2026, https://theenergy.co/articles/more-gas-fewer-poles-and-wires-as-victorian-opposition-looks-to-regional-votes — read live. The Australia Today, 19 Aug 2026 — read live (cost figures: Vic component $3.87bn, combined $7.6bn; ABC cites $7.6–11.4bn — figures are media, not Coalition costings).
- **What replaces it:** (a) gas-firmed "Energy Security Requirement" tenders (§6); (b) "Urban Solar Parks" — EOI for solar+battery on Melbourne industrial/commercial rooftops, $7m to fast-track approvals, aspiration of 25% of demand from rooftops (Stock & Land, 8 May 2026, https://www.stockandland.com.au/story/9242129/ — read live; 25% figure is Bruce Mountain's national estimate, not a Coalition target); (c) review of Transmission Plan and REZs. Davis at CEDA: cabinet subcommittee on energy projects (RenewEconomy, date not verified).
- Mechanism for cancelling: not stated (VNI West is an AEMO/Transgrid–TCV project under national rules; a state can withhold approvals/EES/TCV participation). Funding/offsets: no sunk-cost or compensation figure. Timing: 100-day plan item. Regional distribution: Wimmera/Loddon corridor (Bulgana–Kerang); RenewEconomy notes ~700 construction jobs at risk. **Unspecified:** cost of exit, replacement transmission for Murray River REZ generation, WRL decision criteria, fate of projects dependent on VNI West.

## 4. Landholder rights, fines, compensation [joint, Nationals-led]

- **Commitments:** repeal "compulsory acquisition"/land-access powers that let transmission proponents enter or acquire before approvals; rescind fines issued to farmers who refused access (media cite $814 fines — The Standard, 20 Aug 2026); restore VCAT appeal rights on major projects; agricultural land audits and an "Agricultural Land and Food Security Bill" (Kealy, 31 Aug 2026, https://www.emmakealy.com/media/liberals-and-nationals-to-give-a-fresh-start-for-victorian-agriculture-2/ — read live). Quote: "we reject Labor's plans to decimate agriculture and private property rights" (Wilson, The Energy).
- Source: vic.nationals.org.au/policies/ (read live); ABC 19 Aug 2026.
- **Not found:** any dollar compensation scheme, payment rates for hosting lines, formal "farmer veto", transmission **undergrounding** commitment, or community benefit-sharing framework. Searched "Nationals undergrounding", "farmer veto", "benefit sharing" — no Coalition commitment located. Mark **not verified / not found**.

## 5. Wind-turbine setbacks and renewables planning [joint; Nationals platform]

- **Commitment:** "Reinstate 2km buffer zones" between turbines/renewable projects and homes, "rebuttable by the proponents or the community"; restore third-party VCAT appeals; review REZs. Announced Oct 2024 (Battin era), restated on Nationals platform 2026.
- Sources: vic.nationals.org.au/policies/ — read live; ABC, "Report claims Coalition's renewable 'buffer zone' puts projects at risk", 19 Jun 2026, https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-06-19/report-says-coalition-renewable-buffer-zone-risks-projects/106802794 — read live (CEC: 70% of land unviable, 26,000 jobs, $19m+ council payments at risk; Davis: "balanced approach"). Note Danny O'Brien's long-held view: "onshore wind farms cause enormous community angst" (Nationals release, 7 Dec 2021 — read live).
- Mechanism: planning-scheme amendment. Timing/funding: none. **Unspecified:** whether 2km applies to solar/BESS as well as wind (ABC says "all renewable projects"), transitional treatment of approved projects, effect on VRET pipeline.

## 6. Firm generation: "Energy Security Requirement", coal, nuclear [joint]

- **Commitment (announced 26 Jul 2026, per Tarrangower Times 7 Aug 2026 — indexed/paywalled):** an "Energy Security Requirement" — competitive tenders for "additional new firm electricity generation" before Yallourn closes mid-2028; described by Coalition as "technologically agnostic", with policy text saying "gas-fired generation will be the most likely to provide the bulk of the necessary firming capacity" (ABC, Willingham & Mercer, 23 Aug 2026, https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-08-23/victoria-energy-supply-labor-coalition-election/107065912 — read live). APA Group publicly welcomed it (indexed).
- **Coal:** will not accelerate closures; "letting… Loy Yang A and B running to their end of life"; "We don't need new coal-fired power stations" (Martin Cameron, Nationals, Morwell; release 13 Aug 2026, https://www.martincameron.com.au/media-releases/securing-the-energy-grid-by-backing-coal-fired-power/ — read live; ABC 14 Aug 2026, https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-08-14/victorian-opposition-coal-power-pledge/107033138 — read live). EnergyAustralia restated Yallourn closes June 2028. Davis has demanded the Yallourn closure agreement be published (Latrobe Valley Express, 25 Aug 2026 — read live). No explicit commitment to pay to extend Yallourn.
- **Nuclear:** no state-level Coalition commitment found in 2025–26. Only prior item: Victorian Nationals "open to working with a Dutton government" (2024, indexed). Mark **no current position located**.
- Mechanism: state underwriting via tender (modelled on "schemes already operating" — NSW LTESA-style, not confirmed). Funding: nil; cost to budget unstated. Timing: before 2028. **Unspecified:** MW target, contract form, who pays (bills vs budget), gas fuel supply for peakers, whether batteries qualify (Cameron: "batteries don't generate power").

## 7. Offshore wind

- Position: not opposed in Gippsland; oppose Southern Ocean (Portland) zone; Davis: "adopt a responsible approach" to tenders already under way (*media*: RenewEconomy 10 Jul 2026, https://reneweconomy.com.au/offshore-wind-developers-pray-for-bipartisan-support-ahead-of-key-state-election/ — read live; Gippsland Monitor 10 Aug 2026 — read live; RenewEconomy "go after gas" CEDA piece — date not verified). RenewEconomy notes Coalition's own materials put earliest offshore wind at 2032. No formal written commitment found on party sites. **Unspecified:** support for the 2 GW-by-2032 target, port/transmission funding.

## 8. SEC

- No commitment to abolish or retain found. Davis: "The SEC is a fraud that has not delivered" (Hepburn Advocate/ACM, 24 May 2026, https://www.hepburnadvocate.com.au/story/9252355/ — read live). Liberals opposed the SEC constitutional-entrenchment bill in 2023 (Hansard, 13 Nov 2023, indexed). **Position for 2026: not stated.**

## 9. VRET, emissions targets, net zero 2045

- Wilson (first day, 19 Nov 2025): "There's no plans to change what legislation has gone through the parliament"; "we want gas to be part of that transition"; declined to personally commit to net zero 2045 (ABC, https://www.abc.net.au/news/2025-11-19/victoria-liberals-jess-wilson-first-day/106025500 — read live). No 2026 statement found repealing the Climate Change Act targets or VRET; the Nationals platform has no renewable target. Coalition materials describe firming as "technologically agnostic". **Unspecified:** whether VRET 95% by 2035 and 2035 interim target survive; treat as "not repealed, not endorsed".

## 10. Household bills

- Repeated claim that more gas and fewer transmission lines "drive down" or put "downward pressure on prices" (Davis: "save Victorians significant costs on energy bills"). **No dollar figure for household savings found** — searched "Wilson power bills $", "Coalition bill cut Victoria 2026". Tax items (emergency services tax abolition by Christmas; land-tax threshold back to $300k over four years) are fiscal, not energy. Mark bill savings **not verified / no figure**.

## 11. "Our First 100 Days" plan (20 Aug 2026) [joint]

- Items: Fair Share Guarantee (25% of infrastructure spend regional); recruit 3,000 police; recall Parliament before Christmas for Adult Crime/Adult Time, one-strike bail, abolish emergency services tax, "schools tax", "GP tax"; **cancel VNI West and "throw open the doors to new conventional gas"**; start on one million potholes. Source: Mirage News (republished release), 20 Aug 2026, https://www.miragenews.com/liberals-and-nationals-launch-our-first-100-1730146/ — read live; Gippsland Times 25 Aug 2026 (indexed). Only one energy line; no bills, renewables, drought or Latrobe items.

## 12. Drought, bushfire/CFA, Latrobe Valley

- **Drought:** no standalone Coalition drought package found for 2026. Nationals 2024 call for a "green drought" declaration/mapping (Kealy release, 23 Jul 2024 — read live). Wilson's Feb 2026 farmer pitch (Stock & Land, 18 Feb 2026, https://www.stockandland.com.au/story/9175968/ — read live) centres on scrapping the "$3 billion" emergency services levy, roads, red tape, succession. Kealy 31 Aug 2026 agriculture release: $5bn roads over four years, biosecurity, bird-flu protections — no drought dollars. **Drought support: not specified.**
- **Bushfire/CFA (one line):** abolition of the Emergency Services and Volunteers Fund levy is the Coalition's headline CFA/volunteer item; covered elsewhere.
- **Latrobe Valley jobs:** position is coal-to-end-of-life plus firming tenders; Cameron's releases attack RDV/Morwell job delivery but commit no Valley transition fund or job numbers. **Not specified.** (Latrobe City's welcomed "$1bn/4.5 GW + SEC in Morwell" commitment is Labor's.)

## 13. Regional distribution summary

Wimmera/Loddon (VNI West corridor) and Western District (WRL, 2km setbacks, Southern Ocean zone) get the concrete deliverables (cancellation, repeal of access laws, fines rescinded). Gippsland gets continuity (coal to end-of-life, offshore wind tolerated, conventional gas). Melbourne gets Urban Solar Parks. The only region-wide money is Fair Share Guarantee (25% of new infrastructure) and $288m Country Roads and Bridges ($1.5m/yr × 48 councils × 4 yrs) — regional, not energy.
